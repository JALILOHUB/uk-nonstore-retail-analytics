\# Final Portfolio Summary — UCI Online Retail Analysis



\## Project Overview

Comprehensive analysis of 541,909 transactions from a UK-based online retailer (December 2010 – December 2011), transforming raw transactional data into evidence-based, actionable business insights. This project simulates a real-world freelance data analytics engagement, delivering strategic recommendations for revenue growth and risk mitigation.



\## Business Problem

The company needed to understand its revenue drivers, customer behavior, and market concentration to optimize inventory, improve customer retention, and mitigate the risk of over-reliance on a single market (UK).



\## Key Findings

1\. \*\*Seasonal Dual-Driver Phenomenon:\*\* November 2011 generated £1.5M (2x monthly average), driven by both core B2B restocking (e.g., RABBIT NIGHT LIGHT) and distinct seasonal Christmas products.

2\. \*\*Healthy Customer Concentration:\*\* Validated a Pareto 26/80 rule with a surprisingly low Gini Coefficient (0.142), indicating a broad base of \~1,128 high-value wholesale customers rather than dependency on a few "whales".

3\. \*\*Critical Geographic Risk:\*\* The UK accounts for 84.6% of total revenue AND 90% of top-tier "Champions", creating a dual concentration vulnerability.

4\. \*\*Massive Retention Opportunity:\*\* 47.8% of the known customer base is classified as "At Risk" or "Lost", representing an immediate, quantifiable revenue opportunity (\~£690K annually) through targeted win-back campaigns.

5\. \*\*Operational Revenue Separation:\*\* Identified £362K in operational fees (Postage, Manual adjustments) that must be excluded from physical product performance analysis to ensure accurate inventory decisions.



\## Strategic Recommendations

1\. \*\*Immediate (30 Days):\*\* Launch a VIP loyalty program for the 1,119 UK Champions and a 15% discount win-back email campaign for the 1,188 "At Risk" customers.

2\. \*\*Short-Term (90 Days):\*\* Implement dual-track seasonal inventory planning (pre-build key items by early Oct) and begin international diversification efforts targeting high-value niche markets like EIRE and Netherlands.

3\. \*\*Long-Term (6-12 Months):\*\* Develop guest-to-registered conversion flows to capture the 16.5% (spiking to 23.1% in Nov) Unknown customer revenue, with a strategic target to reduce UK revenue share to <70% within 2 years.
