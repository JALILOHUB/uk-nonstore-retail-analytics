November 2011 generated £1,503,866.78 (highest observed month). Analysis reveals a dual-driver phenomenon: core product amplification combined with seasonal emergence.

Management Implications & Recommendations

🎯

Targeted Retention Testing

The 1,188 "At Risk" customers represent a historical baseline of ~£689K. Design controlled A/B tests for reactivation campaigns rather than assuming guaranteed recovery.

📦

Dual-Track Inventory Planning

Differentiate between core recurring products and seasonal emergent products (e.g., RABBIT NIGHT LIGHT) when forecasting for Q4 to prevent stockouts.

🌍

Geographic Risk Monitoring

With 84.6% of revenue and 90% of Champions in the UK, prioritize data-driven investigation into high-AOV niche markets (EIRE, Netherlands, Australia) for sustainable diversification.
